# Investigate a sales decline

1. Establish that the decline is real using
   [sales review](review-sales-performance.md): matched periods/timezones, metric
   definitions, data freshness and comparable shop/product scope.
2. Locate the contribution by SKU or available dimensions. Separate lost availability,
   listing restrictions, price changes, creator activity and other hypotheses.
3. Test the smallest relevant evidence set: stock analysis, current listing status
   and diagnoses, sales statistics or relationship/campaign context.
   A current snapshot alone cannot prove a historical cause.
4. If traffic/conversion/ad-spend/history is unavailable, state that limit. Do not
   conclude low conversion or ad performance from sales totals alone.
5. Offer an evidence-backed explanation with confidence and a narrow next action.
   Route authorized fixes to product, inventory or Affiliate workflows; never
   execute broad changes as an automatic consequence of a diagnosis.

Keep an optional dated hypothesis/decision memo, not copies of all raw metrics.
