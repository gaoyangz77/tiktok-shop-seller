# Customer service

Choose by the buyer's outcome, not by tool namespace.

- Unanswered buyers, daily inbox triage, a specific buyer or ordinary product
  questions: [triage and reply](triage-and-reply.md).
- Complaints, negative reviews and recovery: [handle complaints](handle-complaints.md).
- Delivery evidence and correction: [delivery exceptions](../order-management/resolve-delivery-exceptions.md).
- Return/refund decisions: [returns and refunds](../order-management/process-returns-and-refunds.md).

Read the selected workflow only. Customer service reuses shared order/product tools;
do not seek Desktop-only, context-locked aliases or inter-agent escalation tools.
